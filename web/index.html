WELCOME TO THE LAB
Every move is a tactic.


Your proof proves Reaches puzzle start: some legal moves turn the starting board into the goal. Each line plays one move, and Lean checks it is legal before going on.


swap 0 1 trades two cells that share an edge. paint 3 blue repaints a cell if paint remains (coral, blue, mint, gold). rotate 2 turns a block a quarter turn clockwise; the small pointer shows its facing.


Cells are numbered left to right, top to bottom, starting at 0. The board follows your cursor: click a line to see the board after it, or use the arrows.


From level 6, name a sequence above the theorem with macro "shift" : tactic => `(tactic| (swap 0 1; swap 1 2)) and use shift as a line. Helpers can call earlier helpers.


The preview is instant but never clears a level. Press Check proof (or ⌘/Ctrl + Enter) and the Lean kernel decides.
